About

Shepherd AI is a product of Astro Rescue Ventures

Astro Rescue Ventures is the registered company behind Shepherd AI. Shepherd AI is not a separate business — it is a proprietary software product developed, operated, and maintained by Astro Rescue Ventures as part of its portfolio of digital products and services.

Shepherd AI is a compliance-first API security scanner built for African startups and fintechs. It scans a target API in minutes, identifies unsecured routes, confirms real data exposure in live responses — not just guesses based on route names — and produces a compliance-readiness score against NDPA, PCI, and HIPAA, the frameworks that govern how Nigerian fintech and healthtech companies are expected to handle sensitive data.

This website, and every service delivered through it, is owned and operated by Astro Rescue Ventures. All payments for Shepherd AI's plans are processed on behalf of Astro Rescue Ventures.
